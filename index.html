Train prototype

Get the train to the EXIT tunnel on the right. You lose if the locomotive is destroyed or every crew member dies.

Fuel runs out while you drive, so you will have to stop. While stopped, the threat meter climbs and attacks get heavier. Moving lets it fall again.

Controls

  • Space or the GO button: start / stop the train
  • Click a crew member (or 1 2, crew cards) to select. Shift adds to selection.
  • With crew selected, click (left or right) the ground to move, a fuel or scrap deposit to gather, or the train to board.
  • Crew can only leave the train while it is stopped. If you drive off, anyone outside is left behind.
  • R recall all crew   Esc deselect
  • Click a car (with no crew selected) to inspect it and repair it with scrap while stopped.
  • A/D or arrows / mouse wheel: look ahead or behind. F follow train. Click the route bar to jump.
  • P pause   H this help

Enemies

  • Shooter: approaches and fires at the nearest crew member or car.
  • Rusher (pulsing, “!”): fast, explodes on contact. Turrets and crew target these first.
