Train

Train prototype

Get the train to the EXIT tunnel on the right. You lose if the locomotive is destroyed or every crew member dies.

Fuel runs out while you drive, so you will have to stop. While stopped, the threat meter climbs and attacks get heavier. Moving lets it fall again.

Controls

  • Space or the GO button: start / stop the train
  • Click a crew member (or 1 2, crew cards) to select. Shift adds to selection.
  • With crew selected, click (left or right) the ground to move, a fuel or scrap deposit to gather, a wreck to salvage it onto your train, a barricade to tear it down, a survivor to rescue them, or the train to board.
  • Crew can only leave the train while it is stopped. If you drive off, anyone outside is left behind.
  • R recall all crew   Esc deselect
  • Click a car (or its chip in the Train strip) to inspect it. While stopped you can repair it with scrap, or shunt it toward the front/rear with Q/E (takes a moment; the train can't move meanwhile).
  • X detaches the rear car, even while moving: lighter train, less fuel burn, but you lose that car (it stays behind as a wreck).
  • A/D or arrows / mouse wheel: look ahead or behind. F follow train. Click the route bar to jump.
  • P pause   H this help

Barricades

A barricade on the track is rammed by whatever car is at the front, which takes the hit. The locomotive takes the full damage; a ram car at the front takes a fraction. Or stop and have crew tear it down by hand (it takes time, and gives scrap). A train that's too slow (e.g. out of fuel) can't ram at all.

Enemies

  • Shooter: approaches and fires at the nearest crew member or car.
  • Rusher (pulsing, “!”): fast, explodes on contact. Turrets and crew target these first.
