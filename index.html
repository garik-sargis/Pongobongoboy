How to play

Get the train to the EXIT tunnel on the right. You lose if the locomotive is destroyed or every crew member dies.

Fuel runs out while you drive, so you have to stop. While stopped, the threat meter climbs and attacks get heavier; moving lets it fall. The land above and below the track is unexplored — scout it for richer deposits, wrecks and survivors, but nests out there wake up when you come close.

Controls

  • Space: start / stop the train
  • Click a crew member or vehicle (or 1–6, or its card) to select. Shift adds to the selection.
  • With units selected, click (left or right): ground = move · fuel / scrap = dig and haul loads back to the train until it's empty · wreck = salvage onto the train · barricade = tear down · survivor = rescue · your sentry = pack up · train = board / dock.
  • B with crew selected: fetch a kit from the armoury car and build a sentry gun where you click.
  • Click a car (or its chip in the Train strip) to inspect it. While stopped: repair with scrap, shunt it with Q/E, or (vehicle bays) V to send a crew member out driving.
  • X detaches the rear car, even while moving. R recalls everyone. Esc deselects.
  • Mouse wheel zooms · WASD/arrows pan · F follows the train · click the map strip to jump.
  • P pause · H help
